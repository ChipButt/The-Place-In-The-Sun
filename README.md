# Roll Britannia: Nassau Prototype v0.13

Mobile-first tile RPG prototype.

## v0.13 updates

- Added proper chicken assets: chicken walk sheet, chicken peck/eat sheet, and egg image.
- Chickens now follow the locked routine: walk, walk, peck.
- After 10 peck sequences, a chicken lays 1 egg.
- One full egg cycle is 150 seconds.
- Chickens continue to progress while the player is away from Nassau, but only up to 3 eggs per chicken while absent.
- When the player returns to Nassau, chicken cycles restart.
- Crops continue growing while the player is in another map.
- Wheat and barley growth is tied to the chicken full egg cycle: 150 seconds total.
- Crop stages advance every 30 seconds.
- Wheat uses yellow/golden crop visuals.
- Barley uses brown crop visuals.
- Crop yield remains 1 planted = 3 harvested.
- Nancy still buys 2 wheat + 2 barley for 5 GP and 1 egg for 2 GP.
